Your own islandin Clew Bay.

A private lodge for up to 22 people on the biggest island in the bay. Twelve minutes from Westport, two minutes by boat, and Croagh Patrick across the water.

0 minWestport

Fourteen minutes from Westport.

Leave town, park at the pier, and the boat does the rest. Scroll to make the trip on a real map of Clew Bay.

Westport, 0 min

Leave the town behind

Head north-west out of Westport towards Rosmoney. Last chance for the shop, the off-licence and a pint in Matt Molloy's.

Rosmoney Pier, 12 min

Meet the boat

Park at the pier and walk down the new pontoon. The team picks you up by high-speed RIB at your agreed time. The return crossing is €20 per person.

Collanmore, 14 min

Step off at the jetty

The lodge is a short walk up from the shore. Bags, kegs and the birthday cake come over on the boat too.

What to bring

Towels, an old pair of runners for the beach, warm layers and whatever you're drinking. Wetsuits and all activity gear are supplied.

The whole lodge is yours.

Set in landscaped gardens a few steps from a sandy beach. No other guests and no shared spaces, just your group, the island and the bay.

Sleeps 22 across 4 bedrooms, with 24 beds and 3 bathrooms.

A kitchen for a crowd

Cook for the whole group with two fridges and a freezer. Or book a private chef and skip the washing up.

Fire pit and hammocks

An outdoor area with a fire pit, hammocks, a big gas BBQ and festoon lights for long Mayo evenings.

Wi-Fi and a smart TV

Fast free Wi-Fi and a smart TV with HDMI, for the match, the slideshow or the team planning session.

Tom's Bar

“Dance as if no one were watching, sing as if no one were listening.”

A replica traditional Irish pub inside the lodge, with draught taps, high stools and bunting. Bring your own drinks, or arrange a keg with the team before you arrive, and the bar is yours till the last song.

Sauna, sea, repeat.

The Atlantic is right outside. Sweat it out with the bay in front of you, run down the pontoon and jump in, then sink into the hot tub.

The beach sauna

Built at the water's edge, with a picture window over Clew Bay and the sea a few steps away.

A hot tub for nine

Covered and outdoors, big enough for most of the group at once.

Steam and seaweed

A steam shower and seaweed baths, and a sea dip cold enough to reset your life choices.

Questions people ask.

    

      
Do we have the island to ourselves?
The lodge and everything in it is yours for your stay. Collanmore is privately owned, with a handful of residents, so expect peace, wildlife and the odd friendly neighbour.

      
Can we bring our own drinks?
Yes, Tom's Bar is bring your own. You can bring a keg too; arrange it with the team before you arrive so it's ready on the taps.

      
How old do you need to be for the activities?
Kayaking is for age 6 and up, and under-16s pay the reduced rate. There are summer adventure camps for kids during the school holidays.

      
What if the weather turns?
The islands shelter the bay, so the team can usually find calm water. If it isn't safe to go out, they'll move you to another time or another activity.

      
Is the lodge open all year?
Yes. Winter stays come with the stove lit, the sauna on and far fewer midges.
